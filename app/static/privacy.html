HeatGuard privacy policy

HeatGuard is a heat-risk warning prototype for outdoor work crews, built by IMADI Tech for the Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge. Last updated 19 November 2026.

What we collect

Only what is needed to send heat alerts: a worker's first name, WhatsApp phone number, role, the crew and site they are assigned to, their language, whether they have opted in, and the replies they send (for example OK or UNWELL). We do not collect health records, precise location, or payment details.

Consent

Alerts are sent only after a worker opts in by messaging JOIN. A worker can reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Deleting your data

Replying STOP opts you out and deletes your stored replies. A supervisor can also delete a worker's replies and opt them out from the dashboard. Deleted replies are removed from our database.

Not medical advice

HeatGuard gives automated heat-safety guidance based on weather forecasts. It is not a medical diagnosis, and it does not replace emergency services or a supervisor's judgement. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Sharing

We use WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta) to deliver messages and Open-Meteo for weather data. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.
